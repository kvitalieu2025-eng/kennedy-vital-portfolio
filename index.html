A little about me

Curiosity
is where I start.

Hi, I’m Kennedy Vital, a second-year Communication and Digital Media Marketing student at IE University in Madrid, originally from Houston, Texas. I love developing campaigns from the first idea to the final piece of content, especially planning the steps, shaping the message, and finding creative ways to reach an audience. I’m fascinated by how customer psychology influences the way people connect with brands.

I’ve produced and hosted podcasts, interviewed energy experts, and created content for YouTube and Spotify. These experiences have strengthened my skills in storytelling, content production, and audience engagement, and I’ve helped grow a digital platform to more than 2,000 followers. I’m excited to create thoughtful campaigns that resonate with people and help brands build lasting communities.
